Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Controller

This app (Habituo – Habit Tracker) is operated privately.
Contact: klaus.workk@gmail.com

2. What data we collect

Habituo is designed for maximum privacy. Nearly all data stays exclusively on your device.

  • Habits, tasks, goals: Stored only locally on your device. Never transmitted to servers.
  • Body data (weight, photos): Stored locally only. Photos never leave your device.
  • Username: Stored locally only.
  • Settings (language, dark mode): Stored locally.

3. In-App Purchases (RevenueCat)

When you purchase Habituo Pro, we use RevenueCat to process purchases. RevenueCat processes:

  • Device ID (anonymized)
  • Purchase history (via Apple/Google)
  • App version and platform

RevenueCat Privacy Policy: revenuecat.com/privacy

Payment data (credit card, etc.) is processed exclusively by Apple (App Store) or Google (Play Store). We never see your payment details.

4. No analytics, no tracking

Habituo contains no analytics SDK, no tracking, and no advertising. We do not create user profiles and do not sell data to third parties.

5. Photo access

The Body feature can optionally use photos from your photo library. Permission is only requested when you actively choose to add a photo. Photos are stored locally and never transmitted.

6. Data storage & deletion

All your data is stored in your device's local storage. You can delete all data at any time by:

  • Uninstalling the app
  • Deleting app data in device settings

7. Children

Habituo is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

8. Changes to this policy

We may occasionally update this privacy policy. The date of the last change is shown at the top of this page.
